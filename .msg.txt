Let an imported word be rated so practice can advance

record_review resolved the word in the bundled lexicon only, so rating an
imported word returned "unknown entry". The practice page then never moved
on: the rating call threw before the index advanced, which is why the first
word of an imported book could not be left.

The lookup now goes through the shared resolver, which answers from either
source. Four tests cover it: the rating records a card, two correct ratings
reach mastery, a wrong answer is counted as a lapse, and a log entry is
written.
