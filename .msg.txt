Add typing sounds, update checking and signed release artefacts

- Typing sounds are synthesised by scripts/make-sounds.py so the package
  carries no third-party audio. A keystroke plays a soft click, a correct
  answer a short chime, and both can be turned off in the toolbar or in
  settings.
- The updater checks the release manifest on start (configurable) and on
  demand from the settings page, then shows the notes in a dialog with a
  download-and-install action.
- Releases are signed, and scripts/manifest.ps1 writes the latest.json the
  updater reads. It writes UTF-8 without a BOM; the first attempt used the
  default encoding and turned the Chinese notes into mojibake.
- scripts/publish.ps1 uploads all three required files, and docs/RELEASING.md
  records the process and the consequence of losing the signing key.
