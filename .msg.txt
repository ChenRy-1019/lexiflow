Enter finishes the copy step instead of grading

Revealing the answer sets `graded`, so both Enter handlers saw a graded
answer and called `commit`. The learner typed the word to copy it, pressed
Enter out of habit, and jumped to the next word — the blind retry the step
exists for never happened. Clicking the button was unaffected, which is why
the flow looked correct in the code.

Enter now calls `finishCopy` while the copy step is open, in the field
handler and in the global listener, and the listener's dependencies include
`copySpelling` so it does not read a stale value.

A DOM test presses Enter mid-copy and asserts the same word returns with an
empty field.
