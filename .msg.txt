Record that publishing needs the author's approval

Releases reach every installed copy, so the version number, the notes and the
timing are the author's call. Building, signing, packaging and committing stay
automatic; `gh release create` and anything else touching GitHub Releases
waits for an explicit go-ahead.

The rule is written down in AGENTS.md so it applies to any agent working in
this repository, and RELEASING.md points at it from step four.
