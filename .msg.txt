Replace the memory percentage with a useful standing

The chip read "记忆强度 100%" every time a word was practised, which
made it look broken. The metric was accurate but misplaced: retrievability
measures decay since the last review, so it is at its maximum exactly when
a word is being reviewed. Probing the values confirmed it reported 100% for
new and mature cards alike.

The chip now names where the word stands in its schedule, using the
stability band the scheduler actually acts on: new, just learned, still
shallow, settling, or solid, with the current interval beside it. The
tooltip gives the review and lapse counts.

Seven tests pin the bands, including that the labels are not all the same.
