Tidy the session size row

Three things made the row look wrong. The goal carried a small badge that
read as a stray label, a half-goal entry produced an odd value below the
others, and choosing custom revealed the field on a second row which pushed
the summary and buttons down.

The options are now the goal and its multiples only. The custom tile turns
into its own field in place, so the row keeps one line and its height whether
 or not the field is open.
