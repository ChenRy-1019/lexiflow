Add CET-4 and CET-6 syllabus wordbooks and the tooling to build them

The published wordbooks people share are often image-only PDFs, which no
parser can read. The official exam syllabi are published word lists, so a
legally reusable source exists: the CET-4 and CET-6 lists compiled in
mahavivo/english-wordlists and packaged by Box611/wordrush under MIT.

scripts/make-cet4-csv.py converts both into the CSV shape the importer
reads, and fixes two defects in the source: a trailing comma that JSON
rejects, and a stray quote in the gloss for hundred. The result is 4540
CET-4 words and 2220 CET-6 words, every one with a gloss and nearly all
with a phonetic.

Three Rust tests check the generated files import, that the word and gloss
columns are not swapped with the phonetic one, and that no row lacks a
gloss.
