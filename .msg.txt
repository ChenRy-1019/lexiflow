Show every word in a wordbook, and let it be searched

Opening a book hid the search box and cut the list at 400 entries with a
footer that admitted so. A 4540-word book therefore showed under a tenth of
itself, and a reader with a word in mind had to page through to find it.

The detail view now pages in 200 entries at a time as the reader scrolls,
reports how many of the total are shown, and carries a full-width search box
that filters by word or gloss. Filtering is in memory because the book arrives
in one command call, so typing costs no round trip.

Four DOM tests cover the search box, the paging footer, a word filter and the
no-match message.
