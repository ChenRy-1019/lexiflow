Show real intervals on the rating buttons and tidy the choices

Every rating button read "< 1h" for a new word, so the four looked
identical and the scheduling seemed to do nothing. The cause was the label,
not the scheduler: the first-review intervals are 1, 6 and 10 minutes, and a
day-based formatter collapsed them into one string. Intervals under an hour
are now shown in minutes, and a non-positive value renders as a dash instead
of a fake interval.

The card's FSRS state is also stored as returned rather than forced to 2, so a
word in a learning step keeps that stage.

The multiple-choice options were unaligned blocks of differing length. Each
is now numbered (matching the 1-4 shortcuts), the gloss sits in its own
column, and the correct answer is marked with a tick.
