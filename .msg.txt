Show the gloss on the review card

Revealing the answer left the review card showing the word and its options
but no Chinese. In the choice mode the answer only existed inside the
options, so once the list was behind you there was nothing to check the word
against.

The gloss now sits under the word in every mode, at body size rather than as
a caption, since it is the thing the learner was just asked to recall. The
reveal test asserts the gloss is present and matches the entry.
