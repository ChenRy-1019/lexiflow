Make the daily goal drive the plan and the session size

The daily goal was saved but never read: the home page and the sidebar
ring both used a hard-coded 20, so changing it to 25 updated neither. The
value is now read alongside the counters, passed to both views, and reported
immediately when the settings field changes rather than only on blur.

The start dialog's word-count options were a fixed 10 / 20 / 30 / 50, which
meant a goal of 25 could not be picked at all. The options now derive from
 the goal (the goal itself, half, double and triple), the goal is marked,
and the fixed 10 is replaced by a free-form custom entry.

Also clamps the goal to 1-200, matching what the field accepts.
