Slim the practice toolbar

The back control was a labelled button competing with the selectors for
attention; it is now a bare arrow at the edge with the label in its tooltip.

The scope selector was redundant: the scope is chosen when the session
starts, so showing it again was clutter that could also restart the session
by accident. It is gone, and the row now separates into wordbook / mode /
count / progress on the left and the audio and display toggles on the right.

Verified with a rendered snapshot of the toolbar at 1400px; the frontend
suite is 105 tests.
