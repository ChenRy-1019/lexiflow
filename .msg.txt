Pin the practice toolbar and add a way to resume a session

The toolbar sat inside the scrolling area, so it scrolled out of reach on a
maximised window. The practice page is now a column: a pinned toolbar, then
a scrolling body that centres the question when it fits.

Leaving practice discarded the queue and the position, so there was no way
back to the words that had not been answered. The session is now saved on
every answer, and the home page offers continue with the number of words
left. Continuing restores the queue, the word and the settings. A finished
round clears the saved session so the button does not linger.

Four Rust tests cover the round trip, and the button was verified against a
seeded session showing 15 remaining out of 20.
