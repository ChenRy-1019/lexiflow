Take the custom count ceiling from the chosen wordbook

The ceiling was a constant, so 6000 meant nothing in particular: it was
larger than some books and smaller than others, and it never changed when the
learner picked a different book.

The ceiling is now the size of the selected book, shown next to the field as
「最多 2,327」. Switching to a smaller book lowers the count with it, so a
count that the new book cannot supply is never left behind. A review is sized
by FSRS rather than by the learner, so the ceiling lifts for that scope.

The saved count is also checked against the book it was saved for, since one
book's count can exceed another's size.

The dialog tests gained a second, smaller wordbook so the ceiling could be
observed changing.
