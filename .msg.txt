Stop two voices from playing at once

Two paths could make sound simultaneously:

1. Warming the audio cache for the first word called speak(), which also
   played it. The automatic pronunciation then played the same word again,
   so the two overlapped. Cache warming is now a separate silent call
   (prefetch), and fetching never plays.

2. A slow download for an earlier word could resolve after the learner had
   moved on. Its fallback to the system voice then spoke over the new
   word. Each request now carries a generation token, and a superseded
   request stops before it makes a sound.

The recording player and the speech synthesiser are also made mutually
   exclusive: starting either cancels the other.

Five regression tests cover the scheduler, including the exact case of the
older request finishing last.
