Start a review straight from the due-count badge

The badge reported how many words were due, but clicking it opened the
wordbook picker. A review draws from every book the learner has started, so
there is nothing to pick; the detour made the badge look broken.

It now enters practice with the review scope across all books, sized to the
backlog so one pass clears it.

A DOM test clicks the badge and asserts the session is requested with an empty
book id, the review scope, and the due count, with no dialog on screen.
