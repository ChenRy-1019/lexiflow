Keep the core wordbook in its source order

The build sorted every book alphabetically, which is right for a dictionary
but wrong for a themed list: the CET-4 core book is ten units that build on
each other, and sorting turned it into an A-Z sprawl. Unit 1 should open with
space / universe / unite, not aboard / abolish / above.

A csv-backed book now keeps the order of its file; every other book is still
sorted.

Verified in the installed 0.2.3: cet4core starts space, universe, unite,
reunite, union, and all 2327 words are present.
