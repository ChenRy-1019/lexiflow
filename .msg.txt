Clean the spelling variants out of the headwords

Thirty entries in the core wordbook were written with their alternatives
inline — `hono(u)r`, `program(me)`, `realise/-ize`. The spelling mode
compares the typed answer with the headword, so entering `honour` was
marked wrong against `hono(u)r` and those words could never be answered.

Each headword now holds one spelling and the alternatives moved into the
gloss as `（亦作 honor）`. Grading accepts either form, and ignores
punctuation, so `up to date` answers `up-to-date`.

Verified in the installed 0.2.4: no headword carries a marker, the order
still starts space / universe / unite, and 2327 entries remain.
